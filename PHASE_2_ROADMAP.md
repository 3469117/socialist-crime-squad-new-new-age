# Phase 2 Roadmap — Engine Conversion

**Status after v0.2.0:** the first terminology and contemporary-world conversion
milestones are complete. This roadmap now tracks the deeper mechanical conversion
planned for v0.3.0 and later.

The next phase should make the setting mechanically socialist rather than merely
relabeling the original left-right simulation.

## 1. Class and workplace model

Add fields for employment sector, union status, workplace militancy, class position, and ownership stake. Recruitment difficulty should depend on both ideology and material position.

## 2. Organizing actions

Add legal/semi-legal activities alongside existing crimes:

- organize workplace
- form/join union
- conduct strike vote
- run strike fund
- tenant organizing
- mutual aid
- canvassing
- public meeting
- legal defense fundraiser
- leak documents
- investigative journalism

## 3. Strike system

Worksites receive organization percentage, employer resistance, strike readiness, and economic importance. Successful coordinated strikes should affect money, public opinion, corporate power, and legislation.

## 4. Ownership model

Separate `Law.corporate` into actual ownership state: conventional private ownership, codetermination, public enterprise, cooperative ownership, and socialized sectors.

## 5. Media simulation

Replace the repurposed AM-radio channel with:

- Legacy Media sentiment
- Algorithmic Media sentiment
- virality
- platform suppression/amplification
- creator/influencer reach
- organization credibility

## 6. Electoral factions

Keep Democrats and Republicans initially, but allow candidate distributions to shift with public opinion. Later reforms can unlock proportional representation and mechanically viable third parties.

## 7. New sites

Priority 2026 sites:

- fulfillment center
- data center
- gig-economy dispatch hub
- private-equity headquarters
- national podcast studio
- influencer content house
- federal immigration office
- police intelligence fusion center
- union hall
- tenant association office
- mutual-aid warehouse
- AI/biotech campus

## 8. New occupations

Priority archetypes:

- warehouse worker
- gig driver
- union organizer
- tenant organizer
- public defender
- immigration attorney
- content moderator
- influencer
- podcaster
- data-center technician
- AI engineer
- political appointee
- federal contractor
- corporate union-avoidance consultant

## 9. End states

Implement distinct victory tiers:

- Social Democratic Victory
- Democratic Socialist Victory
- Revolutionary Electoral Victory
- absurd S+ post-scarcity ending

Implement authoritarian defeat tiers based on election integrity, protest rights, political policing, executive power, and detention policy.
