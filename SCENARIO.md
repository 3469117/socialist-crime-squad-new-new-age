# SCS: 2026 USA — Scenario Specification

## Core premise

The United States begins in August 2026 in a fictionalized, heightened political crisis. A right-authoritarian governing coalition controls the executive and has strong institutional power. The conventional Liberal center remains large enough to matter but is unable to reverse the trajectory on its own. The player's Socialist Crime Squad attempts to move public opinion, institutions, and law toward socialism while a rival Fascist Crime Squad radicalizes the opposite pole.

This is political satire and alternate-history game fiction, not a factual classification of specific real-world officeholders.

## Factions

### Socialist Crime Squad (SCS)

The player organization. A deliberately overconfident clandestine socialist movement whose members range from disciplined labor organizers to catastrophically online weirdos with three followers and a manifesto.

### Fascist Crime Squad (FCS)

The rival organization. It represents the extra-institutional authoritarian pole: violent nationalists, political enforcers, propagandists, patrons, and reactionary organizers. It is distinct from ordinary Reactionary voters and officeholders.

### Liberals

Liberals remain the center rather than disappearing from the simulation. They can defend elections, civil rights, and procedural restraints while opposing Socialist economic transformation or SCS criminal tactics.

## Tone

The intended tone is still LCS: maximalist political satire mixed with systems-heavy simulation and absurd procedural consequences. Socialist victories should range from plausible social-democratic reform through democratic socialism to a deliberately ridiculous S+ post-scarcity endpoint. Fascist victory should progressively destroy competitive democratic institutions and eventually trigger an authoritarian game-over state.
